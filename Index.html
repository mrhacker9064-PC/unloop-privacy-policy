Privacy Policy for Unloop

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application Unloop (hereinafter referred to as the "Game"), a casual logic and maze puzzle game designed for mobile devices.

1. Information Collection and Use

Unloop does not collect, transmit, or store any sensitive personal data. We do not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precision location, or financial details.

In-Game Virtual Items & Points: The coins, points, daily streaks, unlocked themes, and level completions are stored locally on your device via SharedPreferences. These virtual items hold no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed for cash, and involve no gambling, wagering, or real-money betting.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The Game integrates third-party services that may collect device identifiers used for ad personalization, performance monitoring, and analytics:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads (e.g., for extra lives or coins) and interstitial ads. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), general IP location, and diagnostics to serve non-fraudulent and relevant advertisements.
  • For more information on Google's practices, visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Feedback

The Game utilizes built-in Android vibration services (Haptic Feedback) and audio systems purely for responsive tactile game mechanics. These features operate offline without logging or transmitting user interactions.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Unloop is a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If any child-directed data is processed by third-party ad networks, it complies with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA standards.

5. Security

We are committed to securing your trust. Since all puzzle progress and game settings reside locally on your device storage, your gameplay experience remains private and safe.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting on this URL.
